## The 60-second start

1. **Instant writing**: Open and write immediately without mandatory project setup. Press `Ctrl+S` (or `Cmd+S` on macOS) to save to disk.
2. **Three-State View Switcher**: Press **`Ctrl+/`** (or click the segmented pill in the top center) to fluidly switch between **✍️ Edit (Live Preview) ⇄ 📖 Reader (Zen Mode) ⇄ 💻 Source (Raw Markdown)**.
3. **Selection Bubble Bar**: Highlight any text to bring up the floating action bar for bold, italics, code, links, or one-click Catstep AI rewrite.
4. **Typora 3-in-1 Sidebar**: Press `Ctrl+Shift+L` to toggle the left drawer (Files, Outline, and Global Search).
5. **Drag-and-Drop Assets**: Drop images directly into the editor for automatic relative linking or automatic cloud uploader sync.

## What makes Catstep MD special

- **Typora-grade typography**: Refined line-heights and margins with premium themes (Github, Newsprint serif, Night charcoal dark, etc.).
- **In-place Table & Formula Editing**: Move your cursor into any table to reveal floating tools for adding/deleting rows & columns, with standard `Tab`/`Shift+Tab` cell navigation.
- **⏱️ Version Time-Machine (AutoGit)**: Automatically commits snapshots on save; inspect side-by-side diffs and safely roll back changes from the right drawer.
- **✨ Catstep AI Assistant**: Press `Ctrl+J` or `Ctrl+Shift+A` to slide out the right AI drawer for polishing, summarizing, explaining, and interacting with LLMs.
- **Fullscreen Slideshow**: Press `Ctrl+Alt+P` to turn your document into a distraction-free presentation, with `---` page dividers.
- **Local-First & Privacy Focused**: Your notes stay on your local disk with optional transparent end-to-end encryption and private GitHub synchronization.

> 💡 **Tip**: Press **`Ctrl+Shift+P`** (or `Cmd+Shift+P` on macOS) to open the **Command Palette** to search and trigger any command.
